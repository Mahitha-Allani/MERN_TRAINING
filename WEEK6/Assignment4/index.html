OUTDOOR
ACTIVITY

Outdoor activities are recreational activities that take place in natural environments such as mountains, forests, rivers, and beaches. These activities include hiking, camping, rafting, skiing, and trekking. They help people stay physically active, reduce stress, and connect with nature. Outdoor activities also promote teamwork, adventure, and exploration while improving both mental and physical health.
